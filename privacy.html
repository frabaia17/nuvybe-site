Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

nuvybe is a social app for rating music and sharing how it makes you feel. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

nuvybe is developed by an independent developer based in Italy, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at nuvybe.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, username and password (stored encrypted; we never see it).
  • Profile: profile photo, bio, favourite artists and genres, if you add them.
  • What you post: ratings, vybes, reviews, comments, likes, reposts, follows, vybelists and saved vybelists.
  • Safety: the accounts you block and the reports you send.
  • On your phone only: app settings such as light or dark mode, and a cache of music information to make the app faster.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos library (except the photo you pick) or advertising identifiers.

What is public

nuvybe is a social app: your username, profile photo, bio, favourite artists, ratings, reviews, comments, reposts, followers, following and vybelists can be seen by anyone using nuvybe. Your email address is never shown to other users. Blocks and reports are private.

Why we use your data

  • To run your account and show your profile and posts (performing our contract with you).
  • To recommend music and people based on your ratings and vybes (the core of the service).
  • To send account emails, such as confirming your email address.
  • To keep nuvybe safe: reviewing reports and removing content or accounts that break our rules (legitimate interest).

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts our database, accounts and profile photos.
  • Google (Gmail) sends our account emails.
  • Expo delivers app updates; it doesn't receive your account data.
  • Deezer and MusicBrainz provide music information, cover art and 30-second previews. Your phone contacts them directly when you browse music, so they can see your IP address, as with any website. We don't send them your account data.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union, under the safeguards required by the GDPR (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, likes, comments, follows, vybelists and photo are deleted straight away; copies in backups are removed within a short period. Reports you sent may be kept for up to 12 months to handle abuse.

Your rights and choices

  • Edit your profile and delete your ratings or comments at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete account.
  • If you are in the EU or UK you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to nuvybe.app@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Children

nuvybe is not meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
